A typical scenario

Chaitanya Furnishings is a 40-person D2C furniture brand in Bengaluru. Customer queries about delayed deliveries, damaged items and refunds all land in support@chaitanyafurnishings.in — a single shared inbox with no ownership, no SLA, and no way to see which of last month's 300 emails are still unresolved.

They sign up for the Scale-up plan, get a branded page at panchaayat.in/chaitanyafurnishings, and connect it to their existing support email. Every incoming email automatically becomes a tracked case; every reply from their 2-person support team goes back out as an email, SMS or WhatsApp update — without the customer needing to know a new tool is involved.

Within a month they can see, for the first time, that refund requests take 9 days on average to close — and fix it.

The Panchaayat API lets verified companies and enterprise customers create and update cases, pull analytics, and expose one-tap "hot query" actions like DND registration or application-status checks directly to their customers.

Authentication

All requests use a Bearer token issued to verified company accounts. Sandbox keys are rate-limited to 100 requests/min; production keys scale with your plan.

curl https://api.panchaayat.in/v1/cases \ -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_••••••••" \ -H "Content-Type: application/json"

Create a case

Used by your website, app or IVR to file a case directly against your verified account — same object model as cases filed on panchaayat.in.

POST /v1/cases { "reference_id": "BF-LN-77213", "category": "insurance_claims", "type": "complaint", "title": "Cashless claim not approved", "description": "...", "anonymous": false }
MethodEndpointDescription
POST/v1/casesCreate a new case, returns a Panchaayat ID
GET/v1/cases/:idFetch case detail, comments and status history
POST/v1/cases/:id/commentsPost a response as a verified company rep
POST/v1/cases/:id/statusUpdate status: open, in_progress, resolved
GET/v1/exportBulk export cases as CSV or JSON (Enterprise)

Hot queries — one-tap service requests

Pre-built request types your customers can trigger without writing a full complaint — useful on your app or IVR menu. Each returns a tracked Panchaayat ID.

Register DND / stop sales callshotquery.dnd_register

Used heavily against telemarketing-heavy sectors — e.g. Policybazaar, Bajaj Finance.

Check loan / credit card application statushotquery.application_status

Pulls status directly if the company has connected their processing system via webhook.

Check insurance claim statushotquery.claim_status

Surfaces claim stage and next required document, if available.

Request refund statushotquery.refund_status

Common for e-commerce and wallet/UPI-linked refund delays.

Webhooks

Enterprise accounts can subscribe to case.created, case.status_changed, case.comment_added and case.csat_submitted events to sync into an internal CRM or helpdesk in real time.
